Reading the parameters

k0_1 · first transition
Where most of the genome becomes unique. Set by genome size: about log4(genome size).
L2 · second component
Fraction of k-mers that only become unique at the second, later transition: sequence in many copies that are similar but not identical (young transposable elements such as Alu; partly homogenised satellites). Across species it tracks repeat (TE) content.
k0_2 · second transition
The k at which those copies become distinguishable, roughly how long identical stretches between copies are. Younger, less diverged repeats give a larger k0_2.
asymptote = L1 + L2
Fraction unique at long k. The remainder, 1 − asymptote, is sequence in near-identical copies that no k up to 151 tells apart (e.g. centromeric arrays, rDNA, recent duplications).
s1, s2 · steepness
How sharp each transition is.
